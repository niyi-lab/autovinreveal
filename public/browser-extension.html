Built for the way people actually shop for cars

Nobody buys a used car from one listing. You end up with fifteen tabs open across Marketplace, Craigslist and Autotrader, and the tedious part is copying each VIN out and pasting it somewhere else to check. The extension removes that step: it reads the page you're already on, spots the VIN, and puts a check button next to it.

It also adds a right-click option. Highlight a VIN anywhere — a listing, an email, a text message pasted into a browser tab — choose Check this VIN's history, and it opens pre-filled. The toolbar popup will decode any VIN for free if you just want the factory specs.

What it does and doesn't touch

The extension only runs on car listing sites, and a VIN is sent to our servers only when you click to decode or open a report. There is no analytics, no browsing history collection, and nothing is transmitted in the background. The source is included in the download if you want to read it.
