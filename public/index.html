💡 Before You Buy — Smart Suggestions

🔍

Always check before a test drive

Run the VIN before you visit. If the history is bad, you save the trip entirely — and the awkward negotiation.

⚠️

Watch out for odometer fraud

1 in 10 used cars has a rolled-back odometer. A $6 report can save you from buying a high-mileage car sold as low-mileage.

📋

Check the number of owners

More owners can mean more wear, deferred maintenance, or deeper problems. One-owner cars are typically worth more.

🏛️

Verify title status first

Salvage, rebuilt, or junk titles can mean a car is uninsurable or worth 30–50% less. Never skip this check.

🔔

Don't ignore open recalls

Some recalls are safety-critical. A CARFAX report shows open recalls so you know what still needs to be fixed — for free at the dealer.

🤝

Use it as a negotiation tool

If the report shows accidents or issues, use that as leverage to negotiate a lower price — or walk away with confidence.

🚩
