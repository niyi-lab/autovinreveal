Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2025

AutoVINReveal ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: Email address and authentication metadata managed by our auth provider (Supabase Auth). We do not store your password ourselves.
  • Payment data: Payments are processed by Whop. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage data: VINs and license plates you submit, report type, success/failure logs, basic analytics, and cached report files for faster retrieval.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser information, and local storage for session preferences.

How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users and maintain sessions.
  • Process payments and manage credit balances.
  • Retrieve, generate, and cache vehicle history reports.
  • Detect fraud and abuse, enhance security, and improve the Service.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about the Service.

Sharing

We share data with service providers only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and storage.
  • Stripe — card payment processing and billing.
  • NOWPayments — cryptocurrency payment processing.
  • Whop — billing for some legacy purchases.
  • Vehicle-history data providers — upstream sources used to generate your report.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain account and payment records as required for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. Cached report files may be stored to speed up repeat access. You can contact us to request deletion of cached data tied to your account.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access-controlled databases, and hashed credentials. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Choices & Rights

  • You can sign in to view or update your account email.
  • You can clear your local report history on the site using the "Clear all" button.
  • To request access, correction, or deletion of your data, contact us at the address below.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your report history on your device. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Our analytics provider (Google Analytics) may set cookies for usage measurement — you can opt out via browser settings or the Google Analytics opt-out extension.

Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

International Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States or other regions where our service providers operate. By using the Service you consent to such transfers.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date and, if changes are significant, provide additional notice via the site or email.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email us at support@autovinreveal.com
